The Florida Cigar Lover's Guide

Every cigar shop and lounge worth the drive in Florida — organized region by region, city by city: Tampa and historic Ybor City, Orlando and Central Florida, Miami and Little Havana, the Gulf Coast, the Keys, Jacksonville, and the Panhandle.

The expanded edition lists 405 cigar shops and lounges in 149 Florida cities.

Each entry tells you where it is and what makes it worth the trip — the walk-in humidors, the lounges you can settle into, the shops that still roll by hand, and the historic rooms where Florida's cigar story started.

Inside the guide

  • City-by-city listings across all of Florida's regions
  • Ybor City's cigar heritage — the factories and the rollers
  • Little Havana's hand-rolled tradition
  • What to look for in a humidor, a lounge, and a house blend
  • Beginner-friendly picks flagged for first-time visitors
